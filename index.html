About the Mobile Application

The Academic Tracking Mobile application intended for BMU students is designed to help organize and track their academic progress. Users can create and manage academic terms, courses, notes, and assessments while keeping track of deadlines throughout their academic journey. The application is also equipped with search functionality and the ability to view one's academic schedule at a moments notice to help students stay organized throughout their program.
